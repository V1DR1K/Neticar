## Design Specificity Verdict

**Content-specific, visual-system-generic.** Copy is unmistakably Neticar (voseo, "problemas reales", Rosario, real products). The design is the 2025 default dark-dev-portfolio shell: near-black, one violet, Montserrat 800, square cuts. "Technical Archive" is a label, not a design — no index numbers, no plate rules, no record metaphor. Swap in any other agency's screenshots and the page doesn't change a pixel.

## Overall Impression

Competent, accessible, well-voiced page whose concept never materializes. The dark/flat treatment is not a taste problem — it's the system failing its own claims: tonal shifts that don't shift, evidence that hides (projects at 55% opacity), hierarchy that collapses to "big white, small gray, some violet."

## What's Working

1. **Copy discipline**: problem-first, no superlatives, authentic Rosario voseo — most product-specific asset on the page.
2. **Funnel structure**: persistent nav CTA + hero CTA + section CTA all resolve to one WhatsApp channel.
3. **Accessibility engineering**: focus-return in mobile menu, aria-expanded/aria-controls, reduced-motion, visible focus, lang="es", single h1.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Evidence is suppressed, not presented
- **What**: Project images at opacity .55 + desaturation + 98% black gradient; hero has zero visual proof (100% type on black).
- **Why**: A studio's portfolio is its sales argument; here it's a watermark behind text.
- **Fix**: Raise image opacity to ~90%, scrim under copy only, put a real artifact beside the hero headline.
- **Command**: part of redesign build.

### [P0] Flatness is structural, not stylistic
- **What**: Five sections differ by 1-3% luminance; --soft-line is invisible (1.3:1).
- **Why**: The user's core complaint; hierarchy cannot exist where surfaces don't differ.
- **Fix**: Break the slab — one materially lighter or genuinely light section, visible field rules, real depth via contrast not shadows.
- **Command**: part of redesign build.

### [P1] Typography hierarchy is one-note, mid-tier broken
- **What**: Everything Montserrat 800; mid-tier titles silently fall to weight 400 (font:inherit). All prose one gray (--muted).
- **Why**: The tier DESIGN.md's "weight carries the drama" depends on is broken.
- **Fix**: Weight the mid-tier 600-700, two-grays hierarchy, widen label→heading→lead deltas.
- **Command**: part of redesign build.

### [P1] Conversion path leaks at the edges
- **What**: wa.me without text= prefill; no OG image (WhatsApp shares a bare link card); metadataBase points to neticar.vercel.app; footer has no WhatsApp; no response-time promise.
- **Fix**: Prefilled WhatsApp, branded OG card, fix metadataBase, footer CTA + "Respondo dentro de las 24 hs".
- **Command**: part of redesign build.

### [P2] Portfolio cards are dead ends with vague claims
- **What**: Not links; no outcome; no stack; duplicate category "Servicios"; English categories.
- **Fix**: Clickable cards, one outcome line per project, Spanish taxonomy.
- **Command**: part of redesign build.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer)**: Monogram-only brand can't confirm who this is in 2s. Project images dimmed — can't evaluate the work. English categories read as template residue. No outcome language.

**Riley (stress tester)**: Four incompatible date semantics in timeline. 10 technologies at 12px reads as keyword stuffing. Dead-div hover affordance. Reveal starts opacity:0 — printing renders blank sections.

**Casey (mobile, the likely WhatsApp converter)**: Nav CTA disappears ≤800px — hamburger is only escape. Footer has no WhatsApp link — journey ends in dead end. 5 PNG screenshots at 1.4-1.9MB each on mobile data. Menu toggle 42px (below 44px guidance).

- hero::before center rule at 4.5% white — invisible.
- Sticky trajectory intro pins for almost nothing (4 collapsed entries ≈ viewport).
- No skip-to-content link. No print stylesheet. Accordion collapse abrupt.

## Questions to Consider

1. Violet does four jobs — if you could keep only one, which?
2. The conversion channel is WhatsApp (link-sharing) and the trust artifact is the portfolio — why is the shared card blank and the portfolio dimmest on screen?
3. Is "Technical Archive" a design or a rationalization for staying dark? What one material gesture would make a founder feel the archive?
